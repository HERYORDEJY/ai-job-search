## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "cross-functional collaboration", "ownership", "autonomy", "fully remote", "async-first"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (candidate's explicit deal-breakers):
- "fast-paced, high-pressure environment" combined with vague scope (can signal poor planning/culture - probe before applying)
- Any language suggesting heavy oversight, rigid reporting, or command-and-control management style
- On-site or hybrid requirements (hard constraint - see 04-job-evaluation.md Location dimension)

## Management Style Preferences

- **Doesn't work with:** Micromanagement, toxic or political team culture, poor/absent leadership. These are explicit deal-breakers, not soft preferences - weight them heavily in culture-fit evaluation.
